What is the Southwest Florida Resilience Accelerator?

It's the resilience track of Ambition Accelerated, a statewide initiative from the Florida Council of 100, anchored at Babcock Ranch. Its inaugural cohort focuses on companies building climate-resilience technologies and solutions.

Who runs it?

Ambition Accelerated is led by the Florida Council of 100 and its Foundation. Babcock Ranch, developed by Kitson & Partners, is the presenting partner and host for the Southwest Florida track.

What does the program focus on?

Four areas: natural systems modeling; inspections, assessment and certification; resilient real estate and infrastructure systems; and purpose-built planning and design.

Who takes part?

Ten selected founders make up the inaugural cohort.

How long is it, and when?

An eight-week hybrid program running October 13 through December, with two sessions at Babcock Ranch on October 26–27 and November 19–20, 2026.

Where is it held?

Programming is at Babcock Ranch in Southwest Florida, with virtual sessions in between.

How do companies apply?

Applications are open through August 21 at ambitionaccelerated.com/southwest.

How does it connect to Babcock Ranch?

Babcock is America's first solar-powered town and a leader in climate-resilient development, which makes it a real-world testbed for the founders' solutions.

How can I follow along?

Through this site's News page and the Ambition Accelerated channels on LinkedIn, X (@AmbitionAccel), Instagram (@ambitionaccelerated), and Facebook.
